What coaching looks like

  • We start from the 13 money rules: pay yourself first, track what you spend, make your savings earn, and keep life in balance.
  • We look at where you are on each rule and pick the next one to work on. You do not have to do all thirteen at once.
  • You do the work between sessions, using simple tools like the tracking sheet. I answer your questions and help you stay on track.
