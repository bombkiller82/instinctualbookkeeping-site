What I help with

  • Bill paying. On time, every time, from a dedicated account you control, with a written record of what was paid.
  • Mail and paperwork. Sorting, tracking, and following up so important items do not get buried.
  • Budgets and cash flow. A realistic plan built around how you actually live, and a monthly summary so you always know where you stand.
  • Medical bills and insurance claims. Reviewing statements, tracking claims, and following up on what is unpaid or denied.
  • Debt and autopay. Setting up autopay, keeping an eye on what actually processes, and building a payoff plan you can follow.
  • Helping a family member. Support for a parent or loved one whose finances have become too much to manage alone.

How it works

  1. You tell me what is going on. Use the form below or send an email. A few sentences is plenty.
  2. We talk it through. A short, no-pressure call so I can understand your situation and tell you honestly whether I can help.
  3. We put it in writing. A clear agreement and a confidentiality agreement before I see anything sensitive. Then we start.

Your information stays protected

I do not hold or move your money. I work as an authorized user on an account you own, with limited access. Passwords are kept in an encrypted password manager and are never sent by email or text.

Please do not put account numbers, Social Security numbers, or passwords in the form or in an email. You will not need to share any of that to get started.

What I do not do

I am a daily money manager, not a CPA, investment advisor, financial planner, or attorney. I do not prepare tax returns, manage investments, or give legal advice. If you need one of those, I am glad to work alongside the professional you choose.
